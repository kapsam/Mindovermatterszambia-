 About Us
MindOverMattersZambia is a community-driven initiative focused on breaking mental health stigma, promoting financial literacy, and strengthening community empowerment. Guided by the belief that Knowledge is God, Knowledge is Gold, Knowledge is Good, we use education and collaboration to build resilient futures.

    Our Focus
Mental Health: Awareness campaigns, stigma reduction, and youth-friendly resources.  
Financial Literacy: Smart Money, Strong Futures initiative — teaching savings, budgeting, and planning.  
Community Empowerment: Building knowledge hubs, uniting villages, and fostering dignity and opportunity.  
Repository Structure
- `/MentalHealth` → Scripts, guides, and awareness materials  
- `/FinancialLiteracy` → Toolkits, charts, and training resources  
- `/CommunityEmpowerment` → Community hall plans, collaboration guides
